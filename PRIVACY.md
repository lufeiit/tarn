# Privacy Policy

Tarn is a VPN client. It does not provide servers, nodes, or subscriptions.

## What we do not collect

- No account is required.
- No analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.
- We do not receive your configuration, subscription, traffic, DNS queries, or
  connection history.
- Importing a subscription code sends only that code. It carries no device
  identifier, model, OS version, or app version.

## Data on your device

Configurations, subscription contents, selected nodes, and traffic counters stay
in the app container. Subscriptions containing proprietary node types are stored
encrypted and decrypted in memory when used.

A randomly generated installation value is stored on the device. It is created on
first launch, changes on reinstall, is not derived from hardware identifiers, and
is not shared between apps. It lets a server you connect to distinguish
concurrent sessions.

## What the servers you configure can see

Your traffic goes to the server in your configuration, not to us. That server can
see your IP address, the installation value above, connection timestamps, and the
destinations its protocol exposes.

Subscription downloads go to the address in the subscription. Its operator can
see your IP address and the request.
