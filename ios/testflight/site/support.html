FlowArc Support

A general-purpose VPN client for iOS

FlowArc is a VPN client for iOS. It does not offer servers, subscriptions, or accounts.

Getting started

  • Open Profiles and add a subscription URL or configuration file, or create your own configuration.
  • Select the profile, return to the dashboard, and enable VPN.
  • Approve the iOS permission prompt if it appears.

Common questions

Cannot import a profile? Check that the file is a supported YAML configuration or that the URL downloads one directly. Ask the profile provider for a fresh link if it has expired.

Cannot connect? Check your internet connection and verify the server, port, credentials, and TLS certificate match your configuration. Contact the server provider for service issues.

Want to disconnect? Turn off VPN from the dashboard or from iOS Settings.

Contact

Email sp-flowarc@outlook.com for app support. Include your iOS version, FlowArc version, and what happened. Do not send passwords, private subscription URLs, or unredacted logs.
