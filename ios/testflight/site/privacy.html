FlowArc Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-25 · Publisher: XIN XUAN CO., LTD.

What FlowArc does

FlowArc is a VPN client for iOS. It does not provide VPN servers or sell subscriptions. To use the VPN, import a subscription, import a configuration file, or create a configuration, then choose to connect. Opening the app or acknowledging its privacy notice does not start the VPN or grant iOS permission to do so.

What the app processes

FlowArc processes configuration files, server settings and credentials, network destinations, connection metadata, DNS queries, and traffic totals on your device. It uses this information to apply your configuration, route traffic, display network activity, and troubleshoot connection problems. The app may keep configurations, settings, and diagnostic files locally. It does not send your configurations, traffic history, DNS history, diagnostic logs, or usage data to the developer or to analytics services. We do not use VPN activity for advertising or sell it.

Network services you use

Your selected servers and DNS resolvers receive the traffic and queries directed to them. Importing or refreshing subscriptions, downloading resources, or performing network checks may send requests to the services involved. Those services can see the IP address used for the request and the information needed to provide their service. Your choice of configuration determines which servers and resolvers handle your traffic; their own privacy policies apply. The fact that FlowArc does not upload data to the developer does not make your VPN provider or other network services anonymous or unable to log requests.

If you import a signed provider configuration code, FlowArc sends the code and an app-generated device identifier to the provider's redemption endpoint to retrieve your subscription. The provider may use that identifier for device binding.

Retention and your choices

You can disconnect the VPN in FlowArc or in iOS Settings. You can remove imported profiles in the app, remove the VPN configuration in iOS Settings, and delete the app to remove its local app data. Removing local data does not remove records already held by a server, DNS resolver, or subscription provider you used. Contact that provider about its records.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and retain it for as long as needed to handle your request or as required by law. Do not include passwords, subscription links, or unredacted diagnostic logs.

Changes and contact

If our privacy practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. For privacy questions or requests about information you sent to support, contact sp-flowarc@outlook.com.
